ME 103 · Product Realization

Bundt Pan & Cake Stand

I 3D printed a pattern, sand-cast the pan in aluminum and cleaned it up by hand. The stand is a walnut top I turned on the wood lathe, sitting on an aluminum pedestal I turned and threaded on the metal lathe. Then I baked a cake in it.

ME 104 · Mechanical Systems Design · Project 2

Most Accurate Force

Matilda Drewett and I (team name: Flexibility Friends) designed a spring and flexure mechanism to deliver a target force. It won the class Best Design Award for force accuracy.

ME 104 · Project 3

Maximum Impulse, Minimum Weight

With Matilda Drewett and Colin Crown. A motor and lead screw on a wire tripod, built to apply as much impulse as possible. It won the award for lightest design.

ME 102 · Foundations of Product Realization

Pulleys & Ping-Pong Balls

Two builds. A pulley system that went from cardboard to a laser-cut acrylic box, and a hand-cranked ping-pong ball launcher.
