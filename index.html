About Me

A Bit of Background

I started out in a wastewater lab in high school, running titrations for EPA compliance. Since then I've worked on neutrino detectors, solar forecasting, robot teleoperation and a report on data center water use. Most of my time now goes to cost models for atmospheric water harvesting and lithium extraction, in Prof. Carlos Díaz-Marín's and Prof. Meagan Mauter's groups.
